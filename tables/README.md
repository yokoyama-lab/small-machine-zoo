# Tables of the seven new URTMs

Plain-text quintuple tables of the seven new universal reversible Turing machines
of the paper. Each file starts with comment lines (`%`) that describe the machine,
followed by the states, the symbols, the blank, and one quintuple
`[p, s, t, d, q]` per line: in state `p` reading `s`, write `t`, move `d`
(`-` left, `+` right), and enter `q`. There are no halting quintuples; a machine
halts where no quintuple applies.

| File | Machine | Quintuples | In the paper |
|---|---|---|---|
| `urtm3-600.txt` | URTM(3,600) | 1,572 | Section 3.4 |
| `urtm4-41.txt` | URTM(4,41) | 142 | Section 3.3, Table 4 |
| `urtm5-38.txt` | URTM(5,38) | 141 | Section 3.3, Table 4 |
| `urtm6-30.txt` | URTM(6,30) | 160 | Section 3.3, Table 4 |
| `urtm7-28.txt` | URTM(7,28) | 160 | Section 3.3, Table 4 |
| `urtm8-21.txt` | URTM(8,21) | 149 | Section 3.3, Table 4 |
| `urtm85-2.txt` | URTM(85,2) | 153 | Section 3.5 |

The tables are generated by the same tools and from the same construction
parameters as the tables in the Lean formalization. The five machines obtained by
symbol merging are given without their two halting quintuples; the formalization
proves universality for this form as well (Section 4 of the paper). In the merges
of URTM(4,41) and URTM(5,38), three pairs of quintuples become the same quintuple;
each of them is listed once, as in the formalization.

The paper does not print these tables; this directory is where they are published.
In the files of the five merged machines, `H`, `O`, and `G` name the roles of a
block: `H` is the stable role, `O` the origin side of a carry, and `G` its
destination side; `+k` and `-k` are the k-th blocks of Q+ and Q- (Section 3.2
of the paper).
